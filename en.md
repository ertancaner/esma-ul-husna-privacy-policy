# 📜 Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** July 16, 2026

## Privacy Commitment

This application respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. It follows the principle of data minimization, collecting only the information necessary for its core functionality. The developer does not directly collect any personal data.

## Data Collection

The application does **not** collect or store personally identifiable information such as your name, surname, email address, phone number, or location on its own servers.

However, the application uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements. As part of this service, the following data may be automatically collected and processed by Google (a third party):

- Device identifiers or other identifiers (Advertising ID)
- App interaction data (such as ad impressions and ad clicks)
- Diagnostic and performance data

This data is processed by Google for purposes including ad delivery, ad personalization, and performance measurement.

Users can:

- Reset their Advertising ID through their device settings.
- Disable personalized advertising through their device settings.

For more information about Google's data processing practices, please visit:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Local Storage

Dhikr counts, statistics, and application settings are stored **only on the user's device** using local storage (AsyncStorage). These data cannot be accessed by the developer, are not transmitted to any external server, and are removed when the application is uninstalled.

## Third-Party Services

This application uses **Google AdMob** to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process certain information, such as device identifiers and advertising identifiers, for the purpose of serving ads.

All data transmissions are performed over secure connections (HTTPS/TLS).

## Notification Permission

The application may request permission to send notifications for dhikr reminders and Islamic religious day reminders. Granting this permission is entirely optional and can be disabled at any time through your device settings.

## Children's Privacy

This application is **not intended for children under the age of 13**. No personal data is knowingly collected from children.

## Data Security

Data transmitted through the application and third-party services is protected using secure HTTPS/TLS connections.
